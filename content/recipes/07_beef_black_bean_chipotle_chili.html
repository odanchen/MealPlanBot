Beef Black Bean and Chipotle Chili

5–6 servings50–55 minutes

Before you start: Soak and fully cook the listed 200 g dry black beans according to their package directions. Use the entire drained batch in this recipe; cooked yield varies. The meal time below excludes soaking and bean cooking. If preparing them ahead, cool promptly and refrigerate until needed.

Ingredients

  • 850 g ground beef
  • 200 g dry black beans
  • 2 onions
  • 2 carrots
  • 2 bell peppers
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 750 g fresh tomatoes
  • 1 tin of tomato paste (156 ml per tin)
  • 2 dried chipotle peppers
  • 4 g ground cumin
  • 4 g smoked paprika
  • 1 g dried oregano
  • 2 g chili flakes
  • 275 g dry rice
  • 25 ml neutral oil
  • 7 g salt
  • 1 limes

Cook the dry black beans ahead as described below. Blend or finely chop the tomatoes. Soften and mince the chipotles. Rice is optional but included in the shopping list; use cooking water according to its package. Add chili flakes and salt to taste.

Method

  1. 0:00 — Start optional rice. Rinse and cook according to package. Put both chipotles in hot water to soften.

  2. 0:05–0:18 — Chop and brown. Cut onion, carrot and peppers into a small dice (¼-inch, about 6 mm cubes). Brown beef in a Dutch oven, breaking it up. Drain excess fat if necessary.

  3. 0:18–0:30 — Build flavour. Add onion and carrot for 6 minutes, then bell peppers and garlic for 4 minutes. Stir in minced chipotles, tomato paste, chili flakes and spices for 1 minute to bloom the spices (release their aroma in the hot fat).

  4. 0:30–0:50 — Simmer. Add blended fresh tomatoes and cooked black beans. Reduce (simmer uncovered to evaporate liquid), stirring occasionally, until thick enough to mound on a spoon. Add a splash of water only if it threatens to stick. Ground beef should reach 71°C.

  5. Finish. Salt and add lime to taste. Serve as a thick chili or over rice.
