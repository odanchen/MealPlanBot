Beet Turkey and Buckwheat Skillet

5–6 servings45–50 minutes

Ingredients

  • 800 g ground turkey
  • 340 g dry buckwheat groats
  • 550 g beets
  • 1 onions
  • 350 g cabbage
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 2 g smoked paprika
  • 2 g ground coriander
  • 1 lemons
  • 1 bunch of fresh dill
  • 200 g plain yogurt
  • 25 ml olive oil
  • 7 g salt
  • 2 g black pepper

Peel and coarsely grate the beets; thinly shred the cabbage. Cook buckwheat with water according to its package. Yogurt is optional but included in the shopping list. Add salt and pepper to taste.

Method

  1. 0:00–0:05 — Start buckwheat. Rinse and cook in salted water according to the package, generally 15–20 minutes; keep covered afterward.

  2. 0:05–0:18 — Prep. Cut onion into a small dice (¼-inch, about 6 mm cubes), finely shred the cabbage into thin ribbons, and coarsely grate the peeled beets on a box grater. Use a separate board for raw turkey.

  3. 0:18–0:28 — Brown turkey. Heat a large skillet with oil. Sauté (cook in a little hot oil while stirring) the turkey with onion, breaking it apart; season with salt, paprika and coriander. Cook until no pink remains and the turkey reaches 74°C.

  4. 0:28–0:43 — Cook vegetables. Add garlic, beets and cabbage plus 100 ml water. Cover for 7–9 minutes, then uncover and reduce (evaporate) the excess moisture. The beets should be tender with a little bite.

  5. Finish. Stir in dill and lemon juice. Serve over buckwheat with yogurt mixed with lemon zest and a pinch of salt, if using.
